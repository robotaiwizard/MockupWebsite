Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data Unleash Athens collects when you visit our website, book a stay or use your guest account, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the room, dates, number of guests, estimated arrival time and any requests you write to us.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We receive only the payment status, amount and a payment reference.
  • Account information: when you sign in to manage your booking we send a one-time sign-in link to your email and set a session cookie. We don't use passwords for guests.
  • Your choices: whether you acknowledged this policy (and when), and whether you opted in to marketing emails.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider's servers record IP addresses and basic request information in security logs.

We don't collect special categories of data. Please don't include health or other sensitive information in your requests unless it is needed for your stay (for example, accessibility needs).

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data and receive a copy (in your account, use "Download my data");
  • Correct inaccurate data (edit your details in your account, or email us);
  • Erase your data (use "Delete my account"; records we must keep by law are kept only for the required period, then anonymised);
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Data portability: your download is in a machine-readable format (JSON);
  • Withdraw consent to marketing at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

You can also email info@unleashathens.com. We reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr), Kifissias 1–3, 115 23 Athens.

8. How we protect your data

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Card payments go directly to Stripe. Sign-in links and session tokens are stored only in hashed form. Staff passwords are strongly hashed, and staff access is logged. Data is kept to the minimum we need and deleted or anonymised automatically when no longer required.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the version date at the top. Significant changes affecting existing bookings will be emailed to you.
